<h2>Tools</h2>
<p>PgArachne is supported by a variety of tools designed to simplify development, testing, and management of your database APIs.</p>

<div class="tools-grid">
<div class="card">
<h3>PgArachne Explorer</h3>
<p>A powerful, single-file web interface to inspect your API, test functions, and view auto-generated documentation. It works directly in your browser and is included in the source code.</p>
<p><a href="api-explorer/" class="btn">Learn more about Explorer</a></p>
</div>

<div class="card">
<h3>PgArachne Toolbar (macOS)</h3>
<p>A native macOS application that lives in your menu bar. It allows you to manage multiple PgArachne instances, view live logs, and monitor metrics with a single click.</p>
<p><a href="macos-toolbar/" class="btn">Explore Toolbar Features</a></p>
</div>
</div>
