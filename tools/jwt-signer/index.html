PgArachne JWT Signer

⚠ Handle your JWT secret with care
  • JWT_SECRET is the master key of your API: anyone who knows it can sign a token for any database role, including superusers.
  • Do not paste a production secret into a web page you do not fully control. Use this tool with development / test secrets only. For production, save this file and open it locally (it works offline), or sign tokens on the server.
  • Everything runs in your browser. The page forbids all network access (Content Security Policy connect-src 'none'), loads nothing external, and never stores the secret — not in localStorage, cookies, or the URL. It disappears when you close or reload the tab.
  • A token signed here is accepted by PgArachne exactly like one issued by /token, including its role and lifetime. Keep lifetimes short and never share tokens signed with a real secret.
